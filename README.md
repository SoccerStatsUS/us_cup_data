
Info about major and minor Cups played in the United States.

Including:

National cups: 

* US Open Cup
* AFA Cup
* Amateur Cup

League cups: 

* Lewis Cup
* Duffy Cup

Regional Cups

* New Jersey Association Cup
* New York State FA Cup
* Brooklyn Soccer League Cups
* Southern New York Cup

Miscellaneous Cups

* Royal Nestor Cup
* Sultana Cup
